## When to use
Use when vertical lines converge, as with a building shot from below, or a document was photographed at an angle, and someone wants it squared by dragging four corners, then auto-cropped.

## When not to use
A simple tilt belongs in Straighten a photo (straighten-photo). Curved lines from a wide-angle lens are not corrected. To get a clean flat scan of a paper document use AI Document Scan (ai-document-scan).
