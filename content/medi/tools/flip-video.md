## When to use
Send visitors here when a video is mirrored, for example a reversed webcam or selfie recording, or needs a vertical flip. Flips can be combined with a rotation in the same pass.

## When not to use
If the problem is only orientation, Rotate Video (rotate-video) is the direct page. Playing a clip backwards is Reverse Video (reverse-video), not flipping.
