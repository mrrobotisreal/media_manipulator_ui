## When to use
Send visitors here when they have an MP3 and need an uncompressed WAV for editing, sampling or burning to CD. Optional trim, normalise, fade and EQ in the same pass.

## When not to use
WAV does not restore quality the MP3 lost. For WAV to MP3 use Convert WAV to MP3 (convert-wav-to-mp3); for sources other than MP3 use Convert Audio to WAV (convert-audio-to-wav).
