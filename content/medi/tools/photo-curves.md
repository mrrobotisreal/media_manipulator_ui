## When to use
Use when someone wants precise tone or colour shaping with a draggable curve for RGB and each channel, up to 16 points, such as an S-curve for contrast or a faded matte look.

## When not to use
For just black and white points use Levels (photo-levels); for a single contrast change use Adjust contrast (adjust-image-contrast); exposure problems belong in Fix exposure (fix-photo-exposure). If the grade is a .cube file use Apply a LUT (apply-lut-to-photo).

## Related

- adjust-image-contrast: one-slider contrast
