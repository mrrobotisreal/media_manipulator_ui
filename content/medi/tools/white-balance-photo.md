## When to use
Use when a photo has an unwanted yellow, orange, blue, green or magenta cast and someone wants neutral colours, using Temperature and Tint sliders on the midtones. Typical asks: "photo looks too yellow", "fix the blue tint".

## When not to use
If the cast differs between shadows and highlights use Color balance (color-balance-photo). To add a mood colour rather than remove one use Tint a photo (tint-photo) or Split toning (split-toning).
