## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a subtitle file generated from speech: "make an SRT for my YouTube video", "auto subtitles for this clip". Accepts video or audio and returns a time-coded .srt.

## When not to use
If they already have captions and need another language, use Caption Translator (caption-translator). For WebVTT output use VTT Caption Generator (generate-vtt-captions), and for a plain transcript use Transcribe Video (transcribe-video) or Transcribe Audio (transcribe-audio).

## Related
- caption-translator: translate the SRT
- generate-vtt-captions: WebVTT instead of SRT
- transcribe-video: plain transcript
- extract-audio-from-video: pull the audio first
