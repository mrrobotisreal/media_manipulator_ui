## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to change the tone of audio: bass boost, treble lift, vocal presence, a genre preset, or low-pass and high-pass filters.

## When not to use
Hiss and background noise belong in Remove Background Noise (remove-background-noise), and muddy speech in Clean Voice Audio (clean-voice-audio). For consistent loudness use Normalize Audio (normalize-audio).
