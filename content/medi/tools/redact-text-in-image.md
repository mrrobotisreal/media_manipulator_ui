## When to use
Send visitors here when they want personal details in a screenshot or photo covered before sharing: emails, phone numbers, card-like numbers, addresses, or all readable text, with a black box, blur or mosaic. Phrasings like "hide my email in this screenshot" fit.

## When not to use
Faces and logos are not text: use Blur Faces in Photo (blur-faces-in-photo) or Remove Object from Photo (remove-object-from-photo). For hidden file metadata, use Remove EXIF Metadata from Images (remove-exif-metadata). OCR is best effort and English print only.
