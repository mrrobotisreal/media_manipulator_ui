## When to use
Use when someone has a .cube 3D LUT (a colour grade or film look) and wants to apply it to a photo at a chosen intensity and export at full resolution. Typical asks: "apply my LUT to this image".

## When not to use
Without a LUT file, use Curves (photo-curves) for tone mapping or Split toning (split-toning) for shadow and highlight hues. 1D LUTs and very large tables are not supported. A log-to-display LUT on a normal JPEG will look wrong.
