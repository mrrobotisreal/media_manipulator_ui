## When to use
Send visitors here when they have photos or scans of printed pages or handwritten notes and want a searchable PDF and an editable Word document. Fits "turn my notes into text" or "make this scan searchable".

## When not to use
To bind images into a PDF with no text layer use Image to PDF (image-to-pdf). It takes page images, not PDFs, so render an existing PDF with PDF to JPG (pdf-to-jpg) first; it does not translate, and handwriting output must be checked against the scan.
