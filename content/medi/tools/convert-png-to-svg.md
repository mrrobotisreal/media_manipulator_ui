## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a logo, icon, signature or black-and-white line art traced from PNG into real scalable SVG paths: "vectorize my logo", "PNG to SVG". A threshold control sets how much detail is captured.

## When not to use
It traces to black and white and is not meant for photographs or full-colour artwork. To go from SVG to a raster image, use Convert SVG to PNG (convert-svg-to-png). To animate the result, use Motion (motion).

## Related
- convert-svg-to-png: rasterise an SVG
- motion: animate the traced SVG
- remove-background-from-image: isolate a logo before tracing
- png-to-ico: make a favicon instead
