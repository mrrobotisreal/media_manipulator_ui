## When to use
Use when a photo is too dark and someone wants to lift it: exposure in stops in linear light, a gentler Brightness slider, or a Shadows lift that keeps colours. Typical asks: "my photo is too dark", "brighten this picture".

## When not to use
If the photo is bright enough but dull use Adjust contrast (adjust-image-contrast) or Curves (photo-curves). If only the corners are dark use Add a vignette (add-vignette). Pure black shadows with no detail cannot be recovered.
