## When to use
Use when a photo is over- or underexposed and someone wants to correct it in stops, then recover highlights and shadows separately with a histogram. Typical asks: "photo is overexposed", "sky is too bright and faces too dark".

## When not to use
For a photo that is only dull, not too bright or dark, use Adjust contrast (adjust-image-contrast) or Curves (photo-curves). Solid white blown areas cannot be restored. If only the edges are off use Add a vignette (add-vignette).
