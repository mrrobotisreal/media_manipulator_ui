## When to use
Use when someone wants to adjust only the parts of a photo that share a colour, like a sky or a jacket, by picking it with an eyedropper (up to 8 targets) and widening with fuzziness.

## When not to use
If the subject shares colours with the background, use a subject or brush mask in Aperture (aperture). For near-grey photos a luminosity mask works better. To shift a hue everywhere use Change object color (change-image-color).
