## When to use
Send visitors here when they need a WebP image as a lossless PNG that opens in any editor and keeps its transparency: "WebP to PNG", "save this WebP as PNG".

## When not to use
If transparency does not matter and a smaller, widely accepted photo file is the goal, use Convert WebP to JPG (convert-webp-to-jpg). For other formats or resizing, use Image Converter (image-converter).
