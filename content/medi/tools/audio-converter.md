## When to use
Send visitors here for general audio format changes between MP3, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG, FLAC, ALAC, OPUS, AC3 and DTS, especially when they want to choose bitrate, sample rate or channels, or trim, EQ or clean up in the same job.

## When not to use
Pulling the soundtrack out of a video is Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video). Separating vocals from music belongs in Isolate Vocals from a Song (isolate-vocals-from-song) or Remove Vocals from a Song (remove-vocals-from-song).
