## When to use
Use when someone wants to darken or lighten the edges of a photo to draw the eye to the centre, with midpoint, roundness, feather and highlight protection. Typical asks: "add a vignette", "darken the corners".

## When not to use
If the subject sits at the frame edge, a vignette will dim it; use a radial mask in Aperture (aperture). To brighten a whole dark photo use Fix exposure (fix-photo-exposure). It cannot add a solid border.
