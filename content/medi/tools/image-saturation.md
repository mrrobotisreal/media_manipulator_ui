## When to use
Use when someone wants colours stronger or weaker across the whole photo: Vibrance boosts muted colours while protecting skin and vivid ones, Saturation scales all evenly, and -100 gives a grey conversion. Typical asks: "make colours pop", "tone down colours".

## When not to use
For one colour only (bluer sky, less red face) use Adjust image hue (adjust-image-hue) with its per-range sliders. To keep one colour and grey the rest use Color pop (color-pop). If the colours look wrong rather than weak use White balance (white-balance-photo).
