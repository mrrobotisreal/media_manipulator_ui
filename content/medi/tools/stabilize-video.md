## When to use
Send visitors here when handheld footage is shaky or bouncy and they want it smoothed without a tripod or desktop editor. It crops slightly to hide the correction.

## When not to use
Blurry or low-resolution footage needs AI Video Restoration (ai-video-restoration), not stabilizing. For reframing use Crop Video (crop-video), and to shrink the file use Compress Video (compress-video).
