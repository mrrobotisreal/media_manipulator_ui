## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to upscale or restore a short, low-resolution or degraded video snippet and compare several AI models side by side. Fits "make this old clip sharper" or "upscale this short 480p video".

## When not to use
It is not for whole films or sources above 1080p. For a smaller file, format change or cut use Compress Video (compress-video), Video Converter (video-converter) or Video Trimmer (video-trimmer); still photos go to AI Image Restoration & Upscaling (ai-image-restoration).

## Related
- video-trimmer: cut the snippet first
- ai-image-restoration: restore a photo
- compress-video: shrink the output
- resize-video: plain non-AI scaling
