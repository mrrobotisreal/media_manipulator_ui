## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a clip to play backwards, with reversed or dropped audio, or a forward-then-back ping-pong loop. Fits "rewind effect" or "boomerang loop".

## When not to use
Mirroring left to right is Flip & Mirror Video (flip-video), not reversing. To change speed use Change Video Speed (change-video-speed); for a GIF use Convert Video to Animated GIF (convert-video-to-animated-gif).

## Related
- convert-video-to-animated-gif: loop as a GIF
- change-video-speed: speed up or slow down
- flip-video: mirror instead
- video-trimmer: cut a short clip first
