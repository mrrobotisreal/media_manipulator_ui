## When to use
Send visitors here when they want PNG graphics or screenshots as smaller WebP files for a website while keeping transparency: "PNG to WebP", "make my site images load faster".

## When not to use
If the destination does not accept WebP, use Convert PNG to JPG (convert-png-to-jpg) for photos or keep the PNG. For JPG sources use Convert JPG to WebP (convert-jpg-to-webp).
