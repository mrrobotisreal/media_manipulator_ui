## When to use
Use for softening an entire photo: a blurred background image, a tiny blurred placeholder, or a privacy screen over the whole picture. Typical asks: "blur this whole image".

## When not to use
To blur only faces use Blur Faces in Photo (blur-faces-in-photo), and to hide text such as plates or names use Redact Text in Image (redact-text-in-image). To make a soft photo crisper use Sharpen Image (sharpen-image).

## Related
