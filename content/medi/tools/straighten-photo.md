## When to use
Use when a photo is tilted, like a crooked horizon or leaning door frame, and someone wants it levelled by angle or by drawing a line, then auto-cropped to the largest clean rectangle.

## When not to use
For converging lines from pointing the camera up use Fix perspective (fix-perspective-photo). For a plain 90 or 180 degree turn use Rotate Image (rotate-image). To straighten video use Rotate Video (rotate-video).

## Related
- fix-perspective-photo: converging lines and keystone
- rotate-image: quarter turns
- crop-image: custom crop afterwards
- image-resizer: resize the result
