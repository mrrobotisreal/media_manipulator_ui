## When to use
Send visitors here when they want any audio file turned into FLAC, lossless and smaller than WAV, for archiving or a music library. Volume, trim, normalisation and EQ can be applied first.

## When not to use
FLAC does not restore quality a lossy source already lost. For uncompressed PCM use Convert Audio to WAV (convert-audio-to-wav); for small lossy files use Audio Converter (audio-converter) or Convert Audio to M4A / AAC (convert-audio-to-m4a).
