## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a large WAV recording turned into a much smaller, universally playable MP3 for podcasts, sharing or storage: "WAV to MP3", "shrink this WAV". Bitrate, sample rate and channels are selectable.

## When not to use
For other source or target formats, use Audio Converter (audio-converter). To go the other way, use Convert MP3 to WAV (convert-mp3-to-wav). For lossless archiving, Convert Audio to FLAC (convert-audio-to-flac) fits better.
