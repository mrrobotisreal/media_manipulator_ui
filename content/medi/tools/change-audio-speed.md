## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an audio file faster (1.1x to 4x) or slower (0.25x to 0.9x) without changing pitch. Fits "speed up this lecture to 1.5x" or "slow this song down to practise".

## When not to use
To change the key without changing tempo use Change Audio Pitch (change-audio-pitch). For video files use Change Video Speed (change-video-speed).
