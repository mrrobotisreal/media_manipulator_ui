## When to use
Use when someone wants to shift colours around the colour wheel: rotate all hues, or move only reds, greens, blues or a custom eyedropper colour, with per-range saturation and lightness. Typical asks: "make the green leaves more yellow".

## When not to use
A cast from the lighting belongs in White balance (white-balance-photo). To recolour one object while same-coloured things elsewhere stay put, use Change object color (change-image-color) with a mask. For desaturation use Image saturation (image-saturation).
