## When to use
Use when someone wants spots, pimples, dust or small marks removed from a photo: they paint over them and an AI inpainting model fills them from the surrounding texture. Typical asks: "remove a zit from my photo", "clean dust spots".

## When not to use
For a whole person or a large object use Remove Object from Photo (remove-object-from-photo). For overall skin smoothing use Reduce image noise (reduce-image-noise). To repair an old damaged photo use AI Image Restoration & Upscaling (ai-image-restoration).
