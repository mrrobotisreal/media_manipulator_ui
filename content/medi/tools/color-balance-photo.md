## When to use
Use when a colour cast differs between shadows, midtones and highlights, or someone wants a look such as teal and orange, using red, green and blue sliders for each tonal range with luminosity preserved.

## When not to use
For a uniform cast across the whole photo use White balance (white-balance-photo), which is faster. For one creative colour in shadows and another in highlights use Split toning (split-toning).
