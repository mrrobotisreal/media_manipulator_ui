## When to use
Send visitors here for full, non-destructive photo editing in the browser: layered exposure, curves, levels, HSL, colour balance, LUTs and effects, masks (gradient, radial, brush, colour or luminosity range, AI subject), healing and presets, exported at full resolution.

## When not to use
It edits one photo with adjustment layers, so not for text overlays, compositing or drawing. For a smaller file use Compress Image (compress-image), for a transparent cutout Remove Background from Image (remove-background-from-image), and to strip location data Remove EXIF Metadata from Images (remove-exif-metadata).
