## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the singing removed from a song to keep the instrumental for karaoke, singing practice or a backing track: "remove vocals", "make a karaoke version". Only for music they have rights to.

## When not to use
If they want the singer rather than the band, use Isolate Vocals from a Song (isolate-vocals-from-song). It splits only vocals versus accompaniment, not drums or bass. For speech recordings, use Clean Voice Audio (clean-voice-audio).
