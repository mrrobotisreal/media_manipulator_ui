## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a single image (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP) wrapped in a one-page PDF for a form, email or printing: "turn this image into a PDF", "photo to PDF".

## When not to use
It makes one page per image, not a multi-page document. For a phone photo of a paper page, AI Document Scan (ai-document-scan) is a better start. To go from PDF to images, use PDF to JPG (pdf-to-jpg) or PDF to PNG (pdf-to-png).
