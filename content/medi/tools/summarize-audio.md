## When to use
Use when someone wants the gist of a podcast, interview or voice memo: a title, short summary, chapters with times, topics, and who speaks in what tone. Typical asks: "summarize this podcast episode".

## When not to use
For the complete text use Transcribe Audio (transcribe-audio). If the source is a video and on-screen text matters use Summarize Video (summarize-video). For a noisy recording that needs cleaning use Remove Background Noise (remove-background-noise).

## Related
- transcribe-audio: full transcript instead of a summary
- summarize-video: the same for video files
- remove-background-noise: clean the recording first
