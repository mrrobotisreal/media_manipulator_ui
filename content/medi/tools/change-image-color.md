## When to use
Use when someone wants to recolour a specific object, like turning a red car green, by picking its colour with an eyedropper, setting fuzziness, and rotating the hue while shading stays intact.

## When not to use
It rotates hue only, so not for making something white, black or metallic, or changing material. A whole-photo cast belongs in White balance (white-balance-photo). To cut the object out onto transparency use Remove Background from Image (remove-background-from-image).

## Related
- adjust-image-hue: shift hues across the whole photo
- select-by-color: build a colour-range mask
- color-pop: keep one colour, grey the rest
- remove-background-from-image: cut the object out
