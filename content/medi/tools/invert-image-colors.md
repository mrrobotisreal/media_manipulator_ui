## When to use
Use when someone wants a photo turned into its negative, for the whole image or a masked area, with opacity and blend modes for partial, tone-only or hue-only inversions. Typical asks: "invert the colours", "make a negative".

## When not to use
A dark photo or colour cast is not an inversion job: use Fix exposure (fix-photo-exposure) or White balance (white-balance-photo). For a controlled monochrome use Black & white photo (black-and-white-photo).
