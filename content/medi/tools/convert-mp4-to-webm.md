## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an MP4 turned into WebM (VP9 + Opus) for a web page or open-format embed, usually to get a smaller file at similar quality. Fits "make a webm for my website".

## When not to use
For WebM to MP4 use Convert WebM to MP4 (convert-webm-to-mp4). To keep MP4 and just reduce size use Compress Video (compress-video); for adaptive streaming output use Transcode Video to HLS (transcode-to-hls) or Transcode Video to DASH (transcode-to-dash).
