## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the background erased from a photo with a clear subject (a person, product or pet) and a transparent PNG cutout back: "remove background", "make the background transparent", "product photo cutout".

## When not to use
To erase a smaller object while keeping the scene, use Remove Object from Photo (remove-object-from-photo). To hide faces rather than cut out a subject, use Blur Faces in Photo (blur-faces-in-photo). It returns PNG only; for a JPG, flatten it afterwards with Convert PNG to JPG (convert-png-to-jpg).
