## When to use
Use when a photo looks flat or washed out and someone wants more (or less) contrast, via a Contrast slider pivoting on middle grey or tone-based contrast that keeps hues. Typical asks: "add punch to this photo", "reduce contrast".

## When not to use
If the photo is simply too dark use Brighten image (brighten-image) or Fix exposure (fix-photo-exposure) first. For haze use Dehaze in Aperture (aperture). For multi-point tonal shaping use Curves (photo-curves).
