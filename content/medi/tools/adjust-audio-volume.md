## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an audio file louder or quieter by a fixed amount, from 0.1x to 2x or by decibels, optionally with fade in and fade out.

## When not to use
For a consistent target loudness across files use Normalize Audio (normalize-audio). Tone changes such as more bass belong in Audio Equalizer (audio-equalizer).

## Related
- normalize-audio: target loudness
- audio-equalizer: shape the tone
- audio-converter: change format
- remove-background-noise: reduce noise
