## When to use
Use when a photo is grainy or speckled, usually from high ISO or low light, with separate luminance and colour noise reduction and a live preview. Typical asks: "night photo is grainy", "remove colour speckles".

## When not to use
Blocky JPEG compression artifacts are not noise and will not be removed. Motion blur or missed focus cannot be fixed here; for slight softness use Sharpen a photo (sharpen-photo-online). To blur a whole image on purpose use Blur Image (blur-image).
