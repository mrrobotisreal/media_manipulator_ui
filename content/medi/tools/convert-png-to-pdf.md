## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a PNG screenshot, diagram or graphic as a one-page PDF to send or print: "PNG to PDF", "turn this screenshot into a PDF". The image is embedded losslessly.

## When not to use
Transparency is flattened onto white, so it is wrong when alpha must survive. For JPG photos use Convert JPG to PDF (convert-jpg-to-pdf). To extract images from a PDF, use PDF to PNG (pdf-to-png).
