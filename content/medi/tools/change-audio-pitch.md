## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a song or recording shifted up or down by semitones, up to an octave, keeping the same duration. Fits "change the key of this song" or "lower this vocal".

## When not to use
To change tempo while keeping pitch use Change Audio Speed (change-audio-speed). For a karaoke track use Remove Vocals from a Song (remove-vocals-from-song).

## Related
- change-audio-speed: tempo instead of key
- remove-vocals-from-song: karaoke track
- isolate-vocals-from-song: get the vocal alone
- audio-converter: change format
