## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to turn a PNG, usually a photo or large screenshot, into a smaller JPG that works everywhere: "PNG to JPG", "make this PNG smaller as a JPG". They choose the quality, and transparency is flattened onto a solid background.

## When not to use
If transparency must survive, keep PNG or use Convert PNG to WebP (convert-png-to-webp). For other source or target formats, use Image Converter (image-converter).
