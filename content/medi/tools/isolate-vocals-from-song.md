## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the singing pulled out of a music track as a single vocal stem for remixes, covers, lyric transcription or analysis: "extract vocals", "acapella from a song". Only for music they have the right to use.

## When not to use
If they want the music without the singer, use Remove Vocals from a Song (remove-vocals-from-song). For cleaning up speech recordings rather than songs, use Clean Voice Audio (clean-voice-audio).
