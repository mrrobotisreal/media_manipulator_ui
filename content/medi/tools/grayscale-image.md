## When to use
Use for a quick one-step conversion of a colour photo to black and white, or to sepia or vintage for a warm aged tone. Typical asks: "make this photo black and white", "give it a sepia look".

## When not to use
For a tunable monochrome with per-colour sliders, film curve and grain use Black & white photo (black-and-white-photo). To keep one colour and grey the rest use Color pop (color-pop). For other artistic looks use Image Filters & Effects (image-filters).

## Related
- black-and-white-photo: fine-grained monochrome control
- color-pop: keep one colour, grey the rest
- image-filters: other one-click effects
- tint-photo: a colour wash instead of grey
