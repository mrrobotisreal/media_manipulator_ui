## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to edit a real video project: combine several clips, images and audio on a multi-track timeline, add titles, transitions or captions, colour grade or key footage, mix audio, and export an MP4. Phrasings like "edit my video", "put clips together with music" or "make a video with titles" fit.

## When not to use
For a single quick job, use Video Trimmer (video-trimmer) to cut the start or end, Compress Video (compress-video) to shrink a file, or Video Converter (video-converter) to change format. For animating vector artwork rather than footage, use Motion (motion).

## Related
- video-trimmer: one-step cut without a project
- compress-video: shrink the exported MP4
- transcribe-video: get captions or a transcript of the speech
- extract-audio-from-video: pull the soundtrack out of a clip
- motion: animate SVG graphics instead of footage
