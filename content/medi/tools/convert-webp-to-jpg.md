## When to use
Send visitors here when a WebP image will not open or upload somewhere and they want a plain JPG, for example "convert WebP to JPG" or "this image won't open in my editor". It lets them pick JPG quality and flattens transparency onto a background colour.

## When not to use
If the WebP has transparency that must be kept, use Convert WebP to PNG (convert-webp-to-png). For other source formats or extra options like resizing, use Image Converter (image-converter).
