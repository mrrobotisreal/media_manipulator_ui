## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to animate an SVG or Lottie file, draw simple vector shapes and animate them on a keyframe timeline, or export animation as animated SVG, Lottie, React, JavaScript, Flutter or GIF. Phrasings like "animate my logo", "make a Lottie animation" or "edit an SVG animation" fit.

## When not to use
If the starting point is video footage, use Darkroom (content-studio) for editing or Convert Video to Animated GIF (convert-video-to-animated-gif) for a GIF. To turn a raster logo into vector paths first, use Convert PNG to SVG (convert-png-to-svg); it is not meant for photographs.
