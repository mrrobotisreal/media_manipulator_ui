## When to use
Send visitors here when a video plays sideways or upside down and needs a 90, 180 or 270 degree rotation baked into the frames, optionally with a mirror flip. Fits "my phone video is sideways".

## When not to use
If the only problem is a mirrored image, Flip & Mirror Video (flip-video) is the more direct page. Reframing belongs in Crop Video (crop-video), and photos go to Rotate Image (rotate-image).

## Related
- flip-video: mirror only
- crop-video: reframe after rotating
- resize-video: change resolution
- rotate-image: rotate a photo instead
