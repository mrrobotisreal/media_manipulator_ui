## When to use
Send visitors here when an AVIF image is not accepted by an app, editor or upload form and they want a JPG: "AVIF to JPG", "can't open this AVIF". Transparency is flattened onto a background and quality is selectable.

## When not to use
If the AVIF has transparency to keep, use Convert AVIF to PNG (convert-avif-to-png). For iPhone HEIC photos, use Convert HEIC to JPG (convert-heic-to-jpg).
