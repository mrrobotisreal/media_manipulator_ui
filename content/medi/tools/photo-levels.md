## When to use
Use when someone wants to set the black point, white point and midtone gamma of a photo, overall or per red, green and blue channel, against a live histogram. Typical asks: "photo looks hazy and grey", "set levels".

## When not to use
For an S-curve or a targeted tone lift use Curves (photo-curves). For underexposure use Fix exposure (fix-photo-exposure). For a lighting cast use White balance (white-balance-photo); for a one-slider fix use Adjust contrast (adjust-image-contrast).
