## When to use
Use when someone wants to keep only part of a photo: cut out the edges, frame a subject, or crop to exact coordinates by dragging a box. It can be combined with a resize to a target width and height.

## When not to use
To crop a video use Crop Video (crop-video). To fix a tilted horizon use Straighten a photo (straighten-photo), and for converging lines use Fix perspective (fix-perspective-photo). To change dimensions without cutting anything use Image Resizer (image-resizer).

## Related
- image-resizer: scale instead of cut
- crop-video: the same job for video
- straighten-photo: level a tilted photo with auto-crop
- rotate-image: turn a sideways image first
