## When to use
Use for a quick single-click sharpen of a slightly soft photo or scan, or to crisp up a thumbnail after downscaling. Typical asks: "my photo is a bit blurry", "sharpen this image".

## When not to use
For controllable sharpening with amount, radius, threshold, clarity and texture use Sharpen a photo (sharpen-photo-online). For a low-resolution or damaged photo use AI Image Upscaler (ai-image-upscaler) or AI Image Restoration & Upscaling (ai-image-restoration). It cannot fix real motion blur.
