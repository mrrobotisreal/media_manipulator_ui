## When to use
Use when someone wants the gist of a lecture, meeting or tutorial video without watching it: a title, a short summary, clickable chapters with times, topics and the on-screen text. Typical asks: "summarize this video", "make chapters for my recording".

## When not to use
For the full word-for-word text use Transcribe Video (transcribe-video). If the source is audio only, such as a podcast, use Summarize Audio (summarize-audio). For subtitle files use SRT Generator (srt-generator).

## Related
- summarize-audio: the same for audio recordings
- transcribe-video: full transcript instead of a summary
- transcribe-audio: full transcript of audio
- srt-generator: subtitle file for the video
