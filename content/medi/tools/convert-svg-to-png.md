## When to use
Send visitors here when a platform only accepts raster images and they want an SVG icon or logo as a PNG at a chosen width or height, with transparency kept: "SVG to PNG", "export my logo as PNG".

## When not to use
To go from a PNG to vector paths, use Convert PNG to SVG (convert-png-to-svg). For a website favicon, use PNG to ICO (png-to-ico) after rasterising. To animate the SVG, use Motion (motion).

## Related
- convert-png-to-svg: trace raster art into vectors
- png-to-ico: make a favicon from the PNG
- motion: animate the SVG instead
- image-converter: convert the PNG further
