## When to use
Send visitors here when an iPhone or QuickTime .mov file will not play on Windows, Android, a browser or an upload form and they want a universal MP4: "MOV to MP4", "iPhone video won't upload".

## When not to use
If the MP4 is still too large, use Compress Video (compress-video). For other formats or codec choices, use Video Converter (video-converter). For WebM sources, use Convert WebM to MP4 (convert-webm-to-mp4).

## Related
- video-converter: other formats and codecs
- compress-video: shrink the MP4
- convert-webm-to-mp4: same for WebM files
