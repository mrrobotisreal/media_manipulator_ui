## When to use
Use when someone wants to lay a colour over a photo like a lens filter: warming, cooling, sepia, red, green, blue or a custom colour, at a chosen density while keeping brightness. Typical asks: "give this photo a warm tone".

## When not to use
To remove a cast rather than add one use White balance (white-balance-photo). For different colours in shadows and highlights use Split toning (split-toning). To apply an existing .cube grade use Apply a LUT (apply-lut-to-photo).
