## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a video re-encoded as H.265 (HEVC) in MP4 for roughly half the size of H.264 at similar quality, for storage, 4K footage or modern devices, optionally downsizing 4K to 1080p.

## When not to use
If the file must play on the widest range of devices and browsers, keep H.264 with Compress Video (compress-video) or Video Converter (video-converter). Only to lower resolution, use Resize Video (resize-video).

## Related
- compress-video: H.264 size reduction
- video-converter: other formats
- resize-video: lower resolution only
- video-trimmer: cut before encoding
