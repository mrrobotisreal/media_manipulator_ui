## When to use
Send visitors here when they want any audio file (MP3, M4A, OGG, Opus, FLAC, ALAC, AC3, WMA) turned into WAV, usually for editing, sampling or software that needs PCM. Trim, volume, normalisation and EQ can be applied first.

## When not to use
WAV does not restore quality an MP3 already lost. For a compressed result use Convert WAV to MP3 (convert-wav-to-mp3) or Audio Converter (audio-converter); for lossless but smaller, use Convert Audio to FLAC (convert-audio-to-flac).
