## When to use
Send visitors here when a video is too dark, flat or washed out and they want brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, gamma, exposure, shadows or highlights adjusted, or a blur, sharpening, grain or noise look.

## When not to use
Photos go to Brighten image (brighten-image) or Image Filters & Effects (image-filters). For AI upscaling or restoring a degraded clip use AI Video Restoration (ai-video-restoration); full multi-clip editing is Darkroom (content-studio).
