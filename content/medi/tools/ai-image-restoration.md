## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to restore or upscale a damaged, blurry, noisy or heavily compressed photo, optionally enhance faces, and compare model results against the original. Fits "fix this old photo".

## When not to use
For just a bigger copy of a clean image use AI Image Upscaler (ai-image-upscaler). Images already large and sharp gain little, faces it reconstructs are not proof of identity, and video goes to AI Video Restoration (ai-video-restoration).
