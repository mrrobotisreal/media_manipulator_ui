## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a different resolution, such as "make this 720p", "scale 4K down to 1080p" or a custom width and height, keeping the aspect ratio by default.

## When not to use
Cutting away edges or bars is Crop Video (crop-video). To shrink the file while keeping the resolution use Compress Video (compress-video); to restore detail in a short low-resolution clip with AI use AI Video Restoration (ai-video-restoration).

## Related
- crop-video: change framing instead of scale
- compress-video: smaller file at same resolution
- rotate-video: fix orientation
- ai-video-restoration: AI upscaling of a short clip
