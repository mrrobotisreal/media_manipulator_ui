## When to use
Send visitors here when they have a JPG photo or scan and need it as a PDF for an application, form or printer: "JPG to PDF", "save this photo as a PDF". The JPG is embedded without re-compression on a single page.

## When not to use
For PNG screenshots or graphics, use Convert PNG to PDF (convert-png-to-pdf). For a phone photo of a paper document, AI Document Scan (ai-document-scan) cleans it up first. To turn a PDF back into images, use PDF to JPG (pdf-to-jpg).

## Related
- convert-png-to-pdf: same for PNG images
- image-to-pdf: any image format to PDF
- ai-document-scan: clean up a photographed page
- pdf-to-jpg: go the other way
