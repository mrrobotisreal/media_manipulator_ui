## When to use
Send visitors here when they need an AVIF image as a lossless PNG that keeps transparency, such as a logo or cutout: "AVIF to PNG", "convert AVIF and keep transparent background".

## When not to use
If transparency does not matter and a widely accepted photo file is the goal, use Convert AVIF to JPG (convert-avif-to-jpg). For iPhone HEIC photos, use Convert HEIC to JPG (convert-heic-to-jpg).
