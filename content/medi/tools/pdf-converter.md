## When to use
Send visitors here when they want one place for PDF and image conversions in either direction: PDF pages to JPG or PNG images, or an image wrapped into a single-page PDF. Fits vague asks like "convert my PDF" or "PDF and image tool".

## When not to use
When the direction and format are already clear, the focused pages are simpler: PDF to JPG (pdf-to-jpg), PDF to PNG (pdf-to-png) or Image to PDF (image-to-pdf). It does not edit PDF content or merge pages.

## Related
- pdf-to-jpg: PDF pages as JPG
- pdf-to-png: PDF pages as lossless PNG
- image-to-pdf: one image to a PDF page
- ai-document-scan: clean up a photographed document
