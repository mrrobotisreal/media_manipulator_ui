## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a waveform visual from audio: an animated waveform video for a podcast or music clip on social media, or a still waveform image for a thumbnail or album art.

## When not to use
If the audio is inside a video, extract it first with Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video). To put audio onto an existing video rather than generate visuals, use Add Audio to Video (stitch-audio-to-video).
