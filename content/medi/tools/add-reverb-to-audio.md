## When to use
Send visitors here when a recording sounds dry or dead and they want room, hall, plate or spring reverb, an echo or ping-pong delay, or a chorus, flanger, phaser or tremolo effect. Typical asks: "make my vocal sound like it is in a hall", "add echo to this voice".

## When not to use
To make audio faster or slower use Change Audio Speed (change-audio-speed), to make a voice higher or lower use Change Audio Pitch (change-audio-pitch), and to take hiss or hum out of a recording use Remove Background Noise (remove-background-noise).

## Related
- audio-equalizer: shape the tone before or after adding space
- normalize-audio: even out loudness after the effect
- change-audio-pitch: shift the voice up or down
- remove-background-noise: clean the recording first
