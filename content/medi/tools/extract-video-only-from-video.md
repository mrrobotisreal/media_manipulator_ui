## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a silent, video-only copy with every audio track removed, for B-roll, silent previews or re-scoring. Fits "give me the video without any sound".

## When not to use
To keep the sound instead use Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video). To replace the soundtrack with music or narration, use Add Audio to Video (stitch-audio-to-video).
