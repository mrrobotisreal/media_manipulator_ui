## When to use
Send visitors here when they want still images from a video: a frame every N seconds, a set frames-per-second rate, or frames at specific timestamps, as JPG, PNG or WebP in a ZIP.

## When not to use
For a short moving clip as an image use Convert Video to Animated GIF (convert-video-to-animated-gif). To enlarge or clean up a grabbed frame afterwards, use AI Image Upscaler (ai-image-upscaler).
