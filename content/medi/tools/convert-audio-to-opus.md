## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an audio file as Opus at a chosen bitrate, sample rate and channels, typically for small voice or web audio. Trim, volume, normalisation and EQ apply in the same pass.

## When not to use
For OGG Vorbis use Convert Audio to OGG (convert-audio-to-ogg); for broad device support use Convert Audio to M4A / AAC (convert-audio-to-m4a) or Audio Converter (audio-converter) for MP3.

## Related
- audio-converter: MP3 and other formats
- convert-audio-to-ogg: Vorbis instead
- convert-audio-to-m4a: AAC for wide support
- clean-voice-audio: clean a voice first
