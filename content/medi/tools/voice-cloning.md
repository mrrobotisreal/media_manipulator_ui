## When to use
Use when someone wants text read aloud in their own voice: they record or upload 5 to 60 seconds of their speech, save a voice, and have it speak any text in over 20 languages with an emotion control.

## When not to use
To invent a new voice from a written description use Voice designer (voice-designer). To change the sound of an existing recording use Voice changer (voice-changer). To make a voice higher or lower without AI use Change Audio Pitch (change-audio-pitch).

## Related
- voice-designer: create a voice from a description
- voice-changer: convert an existing recording
- clean-voice-audio: clean the sample before cloning
