## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an audio file as M4A / AAC at a chosen bitrate, sample rate and channels, for example for iPhone, iTunes or a podcast feed. Trim, gain, normalisation and EQ go into the same encode.

## When not to use
For lossless output use Convert Audio to FLAC (convert-audio-to-flac) or Convert Audio to WAV (convert-audio-to-wav). For a soundtrack inside a video use Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video).
