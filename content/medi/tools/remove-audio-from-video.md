## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a silent copy of a video: "mute this clip", "strip the sound", "remove the audio track". The video is stream-copied where possible.

## When not to use
To keep the audio as its own file use Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video). To swap the soundtrack for music or a voiceover, use Add Audio to Video (stitch-audio-to-video) in replace mode.

## Related
- extract-video-only-from-video: removes every audio stream
- extract-audio-from-video: keep the sound instead
- stitch-audio-to-video: add a new soundtrack
- compress-video: shrink the silent file
