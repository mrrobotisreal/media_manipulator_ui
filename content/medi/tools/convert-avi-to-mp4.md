## When to use
Send visitors here when they have an old .avi file (Xvid, DivX, MJPEG) that a phone, browser or TV will not play, or that is bulky. Fits "old AVI won't play on my phone" or "convert avi to mp4".

## When not to use
MKV sources belong in Convert MKV to MP4 (convert-mkv-to-mp4) and MOV sources in Convert MOV to MP4 (convert-mov-to-mp4). For any output other than MP4 use Video Converter (video-converter); to shrink a file that already plays fine, use Compress Video (compress-video).

- compress-video: shrink the result further
- convert-mkv-to-mp4: MKV source instead
