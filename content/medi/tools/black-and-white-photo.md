## When to use
Use when someone wants a quality black and white conversion with control over how bright each colour becomes, plus a gentle film curve and grain, or a high-contrast look with a dark sky.

## When not to use
To keep one colour and grey the rest use Color pop (color-pop). For a quick one-click grayscale or sepia use Convert Image to Black & White (grayscale-image). For a photo already monochrome but flat use Curves (photo-curves) or Adjust contrast (adjust-image-contrast).
