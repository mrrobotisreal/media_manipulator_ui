## When to use
Use when someone wants to change how a recording sounds: shift pitch up to 12 semitones with presets like deeper, higher, child-like, older or telephone, or convert the speech into a saved or preset voice with AI.

## When not to use
To have new text spoken in a voice use Voice cloning (voice-cloning) or Voice designer (voice-designer). To change only pitch or tempo of music use Change Audio Pitch (change-audio-pitch) or Change Audio Speed (change-audio-speed).
