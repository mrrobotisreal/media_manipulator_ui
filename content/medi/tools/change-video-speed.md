## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a video sped up (1.25x to 4x, time-lapse) or slowed down (0.25x to 0.9x) with the audio kept in sync and voices at natural pitch.

## When not to use
For smoother motion via AI-generated frames use AI Frame Interpolation (ai-frame-interpolation). Playing backwards is Reverse Video (reverse-video), and audio-only files go to Change Audio Speed (change-audio-speed).

## Related
- ai-frame-interpolation: smoother motion
- reverse-video: play backwards
- change-audio-speed: audio-only files
- change-video-frame-rate: fps without speed change
