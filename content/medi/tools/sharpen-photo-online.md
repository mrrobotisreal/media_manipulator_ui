## When to use
Use when someone wants controlled sharpening: an unsharp mask with amount, radius and threshold, plus Clarity for midtone punch and Texture for fine detail, judged at 100 percent. Typical asks: "sharpen my photo without halos".

## When not to use
For a quick one-click crispen use Sharpen Image (sharpen-image). For a noisy photo use Reduce image noise (reduce-image-noise) first. Real motion blur or missed focus cannot be fixed; for low resolution try AI Image Upscaler (ai-image-upscaler).
