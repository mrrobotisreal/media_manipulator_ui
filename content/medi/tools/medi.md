## When to use
Send visitors here when they ask what Medi is, what the guide or the editor copilots can do, how a prepared job works, or how Medi's edits are undone and reviewed.

## When not to use
Medi is not a file tool. To change a file, use the tool itself (for example Compress Video (compress-video) or Aperture (aperture)); Medi prepares the job and the visitor presses the button.

## Related
- compress-video: a job Medi can prepare and start in place
- video-trimmer: cut a clip, also a confirmed in-place job
- aperture: the photo editor with a Medi copilot
- motion: the animation editor with a Medi copilot
