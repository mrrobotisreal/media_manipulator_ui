## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the pages of a PDF as JPG images to share, post or insert elsewhere: "PDF to JPG", "turn my PDF into pictures". They can render all pages as a ZIP or just the first page, and pick DPI and quality.

## When not to use
For pages that are mostly sharp text or line art, PDF to PNG (pdf-to-png) avoids JPEG artefacts. The output is pixels, so it is wrong when text must stay selectable. To go from images to a PDF, use Image to PDF (image-to-pdf).
