## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to keep one part of a video, for example "cut the first ten seconds", "keep just 1:20 to 2:05" or "shorten this clip". It cuts a single start-to-end range, without re-encoding when possible.

## When not to use
Removing several separate sections, or cutting out a middle part, needs Darkroom (content-studio). A smaller file without cutting content belongs in Compress Video (compress-video), and audio-only files go to Trim Audio (trim-audio).

## Related
- compress-video: shrink the kept clip
- content-studio: multiple cuts and joins
- trim-audio: trim an audio file
- convert-video-to-animated-gif: turn the cut into a GIF
- extract-audio-from-video: keep only the sound
