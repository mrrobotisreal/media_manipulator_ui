## When to use
Send visitors here when they want smoother motion by raising frame rate to 48, 60 or 120 FPS with AI-generated in-between frames. Fits "make my 24fps clip look smooth at 60".

## When not to use
To change frame rate to match a project, or to drop 60 to 30, use Change Video Frame Rate (change-video-frame-rate). To make a clip play faster or slower use Change Video Speed (change-video-speed).

## Related
- change-video-frame-rate: plain frame rate change
- change-video-speed: speed up or slow down
- compress-video: shrink the larger output
- video-trimmer: cut the clip first
