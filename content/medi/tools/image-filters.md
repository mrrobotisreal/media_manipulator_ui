## When to use
Use when someone wants a one-click artistic effect on a photo: oil painting, sketch, charcoal, emboss, vintage, sepia, swirl or barrel distortion, or a simple blur, sharpen or grayscale. Typical asks: "make my photo look like a sketch".

## When not to use
For careful tone and colour correction with layers use Aperture (aperture). For a tunable black and white use Black & white photo (black-and-white-photo). To blur only faces use Blur Faces in Photo (blur-faces-in-photo).

## Related
- grayscale-image: plain black and white or sepia
- blur-image: a whole-image blur only
- sharpen-image: a single-click sharpen
- aperture: full layer-based photo editing
