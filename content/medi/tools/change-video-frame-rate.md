## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a video at a specific frame rate: convert to 24, 25, 30 or 60 fps, drop 60 fps to 30 for a smaller file, or match an editor project's rate.

## When not to use
For smoother motion with AI-generated in-between frames use AI Frame Interpolation (ai-frame-interpolation). To make a clip play faster or slower, use Change Video Speed (change-video-speed).
