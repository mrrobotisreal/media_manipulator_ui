## When to use
Use for turning a photo or scan that is sideways or upside down by 90, 180 or 270 degrees (or 45). The rotation is baked into the pixels and the EXIF orientation tag is cleared, which fixes phone photos that display the wrong way.

## When not to use
For a slightly tilted horizon at an arbitrary angle use Straighten a photo (straighten-photo). To rotate a video use Rotate Video (rotate-video). For keystone or converging lines use Fix perspective (fix-perspective-photo).

## Related
- straighten-photo: small arbitrary-angle tilt fixes
- rotate-video: the same job for video
- crop-image: trim the frame after rotating
- remove-exif-metadata: strip remaining metadata
