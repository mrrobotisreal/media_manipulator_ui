## When to use
Use when someone wants a creative grade with one hue in the shadows and another in the highlights, with a balance slider between them. Typical asks: "cool shadows, warm highlights", "cinematic toning".

## When not to use
To remove a cast use White balance (white-balance-photo) or Color balance (color-balance-photo). For a single colour everywhere use Tint a photo (tint-photo). If the look exists as a .cube file use Apply a LUT (apply-lut-to-photo).
