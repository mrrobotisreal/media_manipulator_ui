## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to add music, a voiceover or narration to a video, with up to three audio tracks, per-track volume, offset and loop, either mixed with or replacing the original sound.

## When not to use
To simply mute a video use Remove Audio from Video (remove-audio-from-video). Multi-clip timeline editing belongs in Darkroom (content-studio).

## Related
- extract-audio-from-video: grab audio from another video
- extract-video-only-from-video: start from a silent copy
- remove-audio-from-video: just mute
- content-studio: full timeline editing
