## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the sound from a video saved as its own file: "get the mp3 from this video", "save the audio of my recording". Outputs MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC or OGG from the first audio track.

## When not to use
It copies the sound as is; to improve it use Clean Voice Audio (clean-voice-audio) or Remove Background Noise (remove-background-noise). For the spoken words as text use Transcribe Video (transcribe-video), and for a silent video use Extract Video Without Audio (extract-video-only-from-video).
