## When to use
Send visitors here when a Matroska .mkv file is rejected by a phone, TV, upload form or editor and they want an MP4. Fits "my TV won't play mkv" or "upload site rejects my mkv".

## When not to use
AVI and MOV sources have their own pages, Convert AVI to MP4 (convert-avi-to-mp4) and Convert MOV to MP4 (convert-mov-to-mp4). For outputs other than MP4 use Video Converter (video-converter), and to make an MP4 smaller use Compress Video (compress-video).

- compress-video: shrink the result
- convert-avi-to-mp4: AVI source instead
