## When to use
Send visitors here when they want an audio file as OGG Vorbis at a chosen bitrate, sample rate and channel layout, for example for a game engine or web player. Trim, volume, normalisation and EQ happen in the same pass.

## When not to use
For the Opus codec use Convert Audio to Opus (convert-audio-to-opus); for Apple devices use Convert Audio to M4A / AAC (convert-audio-to-m4a). Other formats are in Audio Converter (audio-converter).
