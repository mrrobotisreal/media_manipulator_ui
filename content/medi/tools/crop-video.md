## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to cut away part of the picture: remove black bars, trim edges, crop out a watermark or corner, or reframe a clip to square or vertical for social media.

## When not to use
To change the resolution while keeping the whole picture use Resize Video (resize-video). Sideways or upside-down footage belongs in Rotate Video (rotate-video), and still images go to Crop Image (crop-image).

## Related
- resize-video: scale after cropping
- rotate-video: fix orientation
- compress-video: shrink the result
- crop-image: crop a photo instead
