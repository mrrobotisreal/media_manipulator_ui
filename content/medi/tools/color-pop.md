## When to use
Use when someone wants selective colour: keep the reds (or yellows, greens, cyans, blues, magentas, or an eyedropper colour) and turn everything else grey. Typical asks: "black and white except the red dress".

## When not to use
For a clean full monochrome use Black & white photo (black-and-white-photo). For stronger or weaker colours overall use Image saturation (image-saturation). To change the kept colour rather than keep it use Change object color (change-image-color).

## Related

- image-saturation: overall colour strength
