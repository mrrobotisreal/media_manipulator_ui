## When to use
Send visitors here when they want JPG photos as smaller WebP files for faster web pages at similar visual quality: "JPG to WebP", "optimise photos for my website".

## When not to use
If the target app or form does not accept WebP, keep JPG and use Compress Image (compress-image) instead. For PNG sources with transparency use Convert PNG to WebP (convert-png-to-webp).

- convert-webp-to-jpg: go back to JPG
