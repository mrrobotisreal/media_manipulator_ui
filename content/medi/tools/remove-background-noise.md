## When to use
Send visitors here when they want steady background noise such as hiss, hum, fans or constant traffic lowered in a recording, with nothing else changed so they can mix it themselves: "remove background noise", "get rid of hum".

## When not to use
For a ready-to-publish voice track at standard loudness, use Clean Voice Audio (clean-voice-audio). It does not remove intermittent noise or other voices, works in mono, and is wrong for finished music.
