## When to use
Use for cutting an audio file down to one part: a ringtone from a song chorus, the one good minute of a long recording, a sound bite without the intro. Start and end points are picked on a waveform, with optional fade in and fade out.

## When not to use
To cut a video rather than an audio file use Video Trimmer (video-trimmer). To get the soundtrack out of a video first use Extract Audio from Video (extract-audio-from-video). To change only the format without cutting use Audio Converter (audio-converter).

## Related
- video-trimmer: same job for video files
- extract-audio-from-video: pull audio out of a video before trimming
- audio-converter: change format without cutting
- normalize-audio: level the loudness of the trimmed clip
