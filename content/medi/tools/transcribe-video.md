## When to use
Send visitors here when they want the spoken words from a video or audio file as WebVTT or SRT captions, plain text, or timestamped JSON. Phrasings like "get a transcript of this video", "make subtitles" or "what does this recording say" fit.

## When not to use
It gives segment timings only, not per-word timings or speaker labels, and it skips music without speech. To translate captions, use Caption Translator (caption-translator). For audio-only files there is Transcribe Audio (transcribe-audio), and for a summary rather than full text, Summarize Video (summarize-video).
