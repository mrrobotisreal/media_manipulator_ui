## When to use
Send visitors here when they want PDF pages as lossless PNG images, especially pages with crisp text, diagrams or line art: "PDF to PNG", "export this slide as a PNG". All pages come as a ZIP, or just the first page as one PNG.

## When not to use
For photo-heavy pages where smaller files matter more than sharp edges, use PDF to JPG (pdf-to-jpg). To go from images to a PDF, use Image to PDF (image-to-pdf).
