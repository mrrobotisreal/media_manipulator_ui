## When to use
Send visitors here when a video file is too big and they want it smaller for email, the web, social or cloud uploads: "shrink this video", "my phone clip is too large", "make an MP4 smaller". It re-encodes MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV or AVI at a chosen quality level and can scale down to 1080p, 720p or 480p.

## When not to use
If only the format is the problem, use Video Converter (video-converter); to cut a section out, Video Trimmer (video-trimmer) is faster and lossless. For adaptive streaming packages, use Transcode Video to HLS (transcode-to-hls) or Transcode Video to DASH (transcode-to-dash).

## Related
- video-trimmer: cut length instead of re-encoding
- video-converter: change container or codec without targeting size
- resize-video: change the picture dimensions
- transcode-to-hls: build a streaming ladder instead of one file
