## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to turn a short MP4, WebM, MOV or MKV clip into an animated GIF for chat, docs or a reaction image, with control over width, frame rate and palette size. Phrasings like "make a GIF from this video" or "clip to GIF" fit.

## When not to use
For long clips, full-colour footage, or anything that needs sound, a compressed video is better: use Compress Video (compress-video). To shorten the clip first, use Video Trimmer (video-trimmer). To animate vector artwork rather than footage, use Motion (motion).

## Related
- video-trimmer: cut the clip down before making the GIF
- compress-video: smaller, better-looking video alternative
- crop-video: frame only the part you want
- content-studio: edit a longer piece with titles
