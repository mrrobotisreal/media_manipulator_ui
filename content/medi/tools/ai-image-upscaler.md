## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a small or soft image enlarged 2x or 4x with AI-rebuilt detail rather than plain stretching: "upscale this photo", "make a low-res image bigger and sharper". Anime-style models suit drawings. Output is PNG.

## When not to use
To shrink or hit exact dimensions, use Image Resizer (image-resizer). For old, scratched or damaged photos, AI Image Restoration & Upscaling (ai-image-restoration) fits better. Not for text or logos where exact detail matters, since added detail is predicted.
