## When to use
Send visitors here when an iPhone HEIC or HEIF photo will not open on Windows, a website or an older app and they want a normal JPG: "HEIC to JPG", "iPhone photo won't upload". Orientation is corrected and quality is selectable.

## When not to use
For AVIF files use Convert AVIF to JPG (convert-avif-to-jpg). For other target formats or resizing, use Image Converter (image-converter). To strip location data from the photo, use Remove EXIF Metadata from Images (remove-exif-metadata).

## Related
- image-converter: other target formats and options
- convert-avif-to-jpg: same job for AVIF files
- compress-image: shrink the JPG further
- remove-exif-metadata: strip location before sharing
