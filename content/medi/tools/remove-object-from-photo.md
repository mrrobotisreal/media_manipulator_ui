## When to use
Send visitors here when they want to erase something from a photo, such as a stray person, sign or bin, by drawing a box around it and letting AI fill the gap with matching background: "remove object", "erase this from my photo".

## When not to use
To cut out the whole subject on a transparent background, use Remove Background from Image (remove-background-from-image). To hide a face rather than remove it, use Blur Faces in Photo (blur-faces-in-photo). Poor for large objects or regular patterns.
