## When to use
Send visitors here when they want a lossless PNG copy of a JPG photo before editing, layering or adding transparency: "JPG to PNG", "convert my photo to PNG".

## When not to use
Converting does not remove existing JPG artefacts or make the background transparent; for a transparent cutout use Remove Background from Image (remove-background-from-image). To make a file smaller, PNG is the wrong direction; use Compress Image (compress-image).
