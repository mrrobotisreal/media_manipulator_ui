## When to use

An audio-only podcast moment to share as video.

## What a pro does and why

Audio-only posts need something to watch: a static cover or waveform, big captions and a clear title. The voice should be cleaned and levelled first.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Clean the voice (Clean Voice tool). 2) Generate a waveform video with the audio-waveform tool, or put a cover image in Darkroom. 3) Add captions (one AI job) and a title card. 4) Apply the podcast voice chain recipe. 5) Export square (1:1) or vertical.

## How to adjust

Several speakers: add a lower-third per speaker.
