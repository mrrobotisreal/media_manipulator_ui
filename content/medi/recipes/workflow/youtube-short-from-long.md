## When to use

Someone has a long horizontal video and wants a vertical clip of the best moment.

## What a pro does and why

Shorts work when the first second hooks, the clip is under 60 s, the frame is 9:16 and captions carry the story because most people watch muted. A professional finds one self-contained moment, cuts it tight, reframes to the speaker, adds big captions and ends cleanly or loops.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Open Darkroom (or the video trimmer for a quick cut) and keep 20–60 s around the best moment. 2) In Darkroom, reframe with the vertical-reframe recipe. 3) Generate captions (one AI job) and style them with the Shorts caption recipe. 4) Add a punch-in zoom on the key line. 5) Export 1080×1920 MP4. Offer `open_tool` for `content-studio`.

## How to adjust

No time to edit: the trimmer plus the crop-video tool give a quick vertical cut without captions.
