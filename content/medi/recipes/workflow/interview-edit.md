## When to use

One or two people talking to camera.

## What a pro does and why

Professional interview edits remove false starts, use J and L cuts between answers, match cameras, introduce the speaker with a lower-third, clean the voice and duck any music.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Transcribe to find the good answers. 2) In Darkroom, cut the false starts (split-and-delete recipe). 3) Match the cameras. 4) Add J/L cuts. 5) Lower-third for the speaker. 6) Podcast voice chain and music ducking. 7) Captions.

## How to adjust

Short on time: steps 2, 5 and 6 give most of the polish.
