## When to use

Voice recordings with hum, hiss, room echo or background sounds.

## What a pro does and why

Use the gentlest tool that fixes the problem: noise removal for steady hiss and hum, Clean Voice for speech enhancement with echo and noise, vocal isolation when music is mixed under the voice. Normalise loudness last.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Try Clean Voice first for speech. 2) If music is underneath, isolate vocals. 3) Normalise audio. 4) In Darkroom, the podcast voice chain.

## How to adjust

Sounds robotic: use the milder background-noise tool instead.
