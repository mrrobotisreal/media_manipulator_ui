## When to use

A video must load fast on a page or go out in an email.

## What a pro does and why

Web video should be short, muted for autoplay backgrounds, MP4 (H.264) for compatibility plus WebM for size, and well under a few megabytes for hero backgrounds. Email clients rarely play video: use a GIF preview linking to the video. Long videos stream better as HLS.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Trim to the essential part. 2) Compress video (lower resolution to 720p for backgrounds). 3) Convert a WebM copy. 4) For email, convert a 3–5 s GIF. 5) For long videos, transcode to HLS.

## How to adjust

Still too heavy: shorter clip or lower resolution; quality settings matter more than format.
