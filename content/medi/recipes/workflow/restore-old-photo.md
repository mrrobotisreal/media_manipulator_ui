## When to use

Scans of faded, scratched or small old prints.

## What a pro does and why

The order matters: AI restoration first (faces, scratches), then upscale if small, then tone and colour in Aperture (levels for black and white points, white balance for the yellow cast), then heal remaining spots.

## Ops

Steps: 1) AI image restoration. 2) AI upscaler if the scan is small. 3) In Aperture: levels-black-white and white-balance-fix recipes. 4) Heal spots. 5) Black-and-white classic recipe if colour is unrecoverable.

## How to adjust

Faces look artificial after restoration: use a milder model or blend with the original.
