## When to use

Many short clips from a trip, set to a song.

## What a pro does and why

Montages cut on the beat, keep shots 1 to 3 s, open with the most striking shot, use one consistent look, speed-ramp a few moments and fade the music at the end.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Put the music on an audio track and drop markers on the beats (M key). 2) Trim clips to land cuts on markers. 3) Apply one look to all clips. 4) Add a title card. 5) Slow-motion one or two hero shots. 6) Fade the music out.

## How to adjust

Feels rushed: hold shots for 2 beats. Feels slow: cut every beat in the chorus.
