## When to use

A short branded intro at the start of every video.

## What a pro does and why

Good intros are 2 to 4 s, built from the vector logo, with a sound sting and the same colours as the channel. Make it once in Motion, export MP4 (or WebM with transparency for overlays), then drop it into Darkroom.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Vectorize the logo if it is a PNG. 2) Open Motion and use the logo draw-on or logo reveal recipe. 3) Export MP4 or WebM. 4) Import it into Darkroom at the start of each project.

## How to adjust

Too long for Shorts: 1.5 s, or skip the intro on Shorts.
