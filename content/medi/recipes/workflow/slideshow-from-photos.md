## When to use

A set of photos for a video with music.

## What a pro does and why

Each photo holds 3 to 5 s with a slow Ken Burns move, cross dissolves of about 1 s, one consistent look and music faded at the end.

## Ops

Steps: 1) Edit the photos in Aperture if needed (auto-fix recipe). 2) Import them into Darkroom as stills. 3) Ken Burns each photo. 4) Cross dissolves. 5) Music with fades. 6) Title and end card.

## How to adjust

Too long: 3 s per photo. Mixed portrait and landscape photos: use a 16:9 canvas and scale portraits to fit with a blurred copy behind.
